How it works

Ask

I put your buyers' 30 questions to OpenAI's search, the way a buyer would. Each question is asked more than once, because AI answers change from one run to the next.

Find the gaps

For every question: were you named, who was named instead, and which sites were cited. The questions where you are missing show where to start.

Fix the pages

You get answer-page briefs your team can publish, a checklist for your developer, and notes on the outside sites the AI trusts.

Delivered as Word files, or Google Docs if you prefer.

What I can and can't promise

The goal is to get you cited by AI search. I can't control what the AI says, so I don't promise it. What I do is find exactly why you are missing and give you the pages and fixes that give you the best chance.

What I measure is OpenAI's search. That is not the ChatGPT app, Google's AI Overviews or other engines, which can answer differently.
